VAULT.PRIVACY

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Vault23 ("we," "our," or "us") is a personal sneaker vault and community platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when using Vault23.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account we collect your email address, display name, and optional profile photo. You may also provide a bio, location, and shoe size to personalise your experience.

Content You Create

Photos you post, sneakers you add to your closet or wishlist, listings you publish, and messages you send are stored so you and other users can access them.

Usage Data

We collect standard app usage information such as pages visited and features used. This data is used only to improve the app experience and is never sold to third parties.

How We Use Your Data

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Vault23 app
  • Display your public profile, closet, and listings to other users
  • Send push notifications for drops and messages (only if you grant permission)
  • Personalise your feed and search results based on your shoe size and preferences
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data may be shared with the following parties only as necessary to operate the app:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database, storage, and analytics
  • Apple — Push notification delivery via APNs

All third-party services are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable data protection law.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all information stored on your profile at any time.
  • Correction: Update your username, bio, photo, and preferences in Settings.
  • Deletion: You may request full account deletion by contacting us. All your posts, closet data, and messages will be permanently removed.
  • Portability: Contact us to request an export of your data.

Data Security

All data is stored securely in Google Firebase with access controls enforced by Firestore Security Rules. Passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text. Communications between the app and our servers are encrypted using TLS.

Children's Privacy

Vault23 is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
